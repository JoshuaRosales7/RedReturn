# Scientific method, assumptions and validation

> RedReturn is a research/education decision-support prototype. It **cannot certify** a route or mission. "Terrain-safe" is never claimed; "feasible within model" means only that stated assumptions pass stated constraints.

## 1 Geometry
Spherical Mars, R = 3,396.19 km (the MOLA grid's sphere). Planetocentric latitude, east-positive longitude wrapped to [−180,180). Haversine distance; great-circle interpolation by spherical slerp. Tests: identity, 1° = Rπ/180, antimeridian shortest arc, pole, wrap vectors, finite interpolation near poles. Python and TS agree on shared golden vectors.

## 2 DEM sampling
Pixel-edge coordinates; centres at +0.5. A query at a cell centre returns the cell (`cell_value`); otherwise bilinear from four cells (`interpolated`). Any nodata neighbour ⇒ `nodata` (no value). Outside the cached window ⇒ `outside_coverage`. A test compares our georeferencing and values with an independent `rasterio` read at four cells.
**Slope:** Horn (1981) 3×3 gradient with latitude-dependent east-west spacing; NaN if any neighbour or the cell itself is missing. Baseline ≈ 1.4 km, so rover-scale slopes are unobservable. Plane test: gradient 0.1 ⇒ 5.71° ± 0.02°.
**Resolution disclosure** (returned by every terrain call): 463 m grid, ≥ 926 m features, ±3 m vertical.

## 3 Route planning
8-neighbour A* on the native-resolution window, corridor search widening 40→120→400 cells, ≤ 180 k cells. A cell is impassable if: elevation missing, local Horn slope unknown or > cap, step slope > cap, inside an obstruction. The heuristic is Euclidean ground distance × minimum per-metre cost (admissible).
| Strategy | Objective |
|---|---|
| shortest | great-circle line sampled on the DEM; **not optimised**; violations are reported |
| terrain | min Σ d·(1+(s/8°)²), s = max(step, local slope) |
| resource | min Σ (E/E₀ + O₂/O₂₀ + W/W₀) per step incl. slope-derated speed (v = v₀·clamp(1−s/s_max, 0.25, 1)) and climb energy (m·g·Δz/η) |
| science | visit declared stops (best order) with terrain-style legs; **downgraded to terrain with an explanation when no stops are declared** because no science-value dataset is integrated |
Blocked ⇒ typed error `no_traversable_route`, never a straight-line fallback.

## 4 Mission model (single authoritative path)
`project_mission` (NumPy; scalar or array): travel time = slope-derated distance / speed; duration = travel + science + delay; contingency multiplies projected use; energy = (duration·P_base + climb energy)·(1+c); O₂ = duration·crew·rate·(1+c); water analogous; dose = duration·rate. Constraints: remaining ≥ reserve% of capacity (oxygen, energy, water), dose ≤ limit, elapsed ≤ window. Margins are normalised; the limiting factor is the minimum. Return retraces the outbound path (ascent/descent swap). Tests: accounting identities, return doubling, reserve boundary (±0.1 %), impossible missions, climb-energy formula, terrain increases time and energy.
Golden vectors (`tests/golden/mission_vectors.json`, generated by Python) are asserted by both pytest and Vitest; CI fails if they drift.

## 5 Uncertainty (Monte Carlo)
Seeded PCG64; seed derived from SHA-256(request) XOR user seed ⇒ identical requests give identical results (`result_sha256` tested). Nine inputs with assumed laws (table in UI). Inputs are classed **measurement/resolution**, **model** or **scenario**; each class is re-run alone to show how much of the margin spread it causes. Sensitivity = Spearman rank correlation and a P10→P90 one-at-a-time swing. Reported: fraction of runs satisfying every constraint with a **Wilson 95 % interval (sampling error only)**, dominant failure mode, P10/P50/P90 of time, resources, dose and limiting margin.
**Not claimed:** the fraction is not a probability of mission success or crew survival; the distributions are not empirically calibrated; quantiles are of simulated outcomes, not confidence intervals on truth.

## 6 Emergency engine
State at the incident = original plan's cumulative consumption (same contingency convention, so a zero-size incident reproduces the original — tested). Incidents: battery capacity loss (reserve threshold kept at original capacity), speed loss, O₂ rate increase, water loss, extended science, delay, communications reduction (**assumption**: 1 h delay per 100 %; the model has no comms physics), route obstruction (circular impassable mask, replanned). Options evaluated: continue as planned (= FAILURE), continue around obstruction, continue skipping science, abort to base (best of resource/terrain replans). REVISED = feasible option with the largest limiting margin; otherwise the least-deficit option with **required additional resources** reported. Levers (skip science, shed 20 % load, recover delay) are recomputed, not asserted.

## 7 Validation performed
74 pytest + 23 Vitest cases (see `VERIFICATION.md`). Real-data checks: Jezero reference point −2.64 km (expected ≈ −2.5 km); independent rasterio cross-check; Jezero window range −4865…+1660 m. **Not done:** comparison against rover odometry/slope data, independent code review, HiRISE cross-check of slope hazards, field validation of resource rates.

## 8 Known limitations
463 m resolution; no rocks; constant rates; no thermal/solar/dust/comms physics; two DEM windows; slope cap is a scenario parameter; routes are cell-centre polylines (staircase) not smoothed; interpolation only along the shortest line.
